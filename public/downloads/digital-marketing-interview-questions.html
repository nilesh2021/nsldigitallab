Digital Marketing Interview Questions for Freshers

Short answers for internship and junior marketing interviews. Cover channels, metrics, and one campaign story.

Basics

What is digital marketing?

Using online channels — search, social, email, ads, content, and a website — to attract, convert, and keep customers.

Organic vs paid traffic?

Organic is unpaid (SEO, content, social posts). Paid is when you buy attention (Google Ads, Meta Ads). Most small businesses need both, starting with a clear offer.

What is a marketing funnel?

The path from first awareness to action: people discover you, consider you, then convert. Your content and ads should match the stage.

What is a CTA?

A call to action: the next step you want, such as “Book a call” or “Download the checklist.” One page, one main CTA.

Metrics

What is CTR?

Click-through rate: clicks divided by impressions. It tells you if the ad or title is interesting, not whether it makes money.

What is CPC and CPA?

CPC is cost per click. CPA is cost per acquisition (lead or sale). CPA is usually the number a business cares about more.

What is ROAS?

Return on ad spend: revenue from ads divided by ad cost. “We spent ₹10,000 and made ₹40,000” is a 4x ROAS.

What would you look at in GA4 as an intern?

Where traffic comes from, which pages people use, and whether key events (form submit, purchase) fire. Then ask which of those events the business actually wants.

Campaign thinking

How would you plan a 30-day campaign for a small business?

Goal, audience, offer, one primary channel, landing page, budget, and one weekly metric. Do not launch seven channels in week one.

Google Ads vs Meta Ads?

Search captures existing demand (“plumber near me”). Meta creates demand with images and targeting. Match the channel to whether people are already searching.

Tell me about a campaign or project

Use STAR: situation, task, what you did, result (even a class project or college fest). Numbers help: reach, clicks, sign-ups.
