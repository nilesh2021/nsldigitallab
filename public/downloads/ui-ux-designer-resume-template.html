Resume Tips

  • Keep the resume to one page for students and early careers, and to two pages only when the extra roles are relevant.
  • Use measurable achievements where you have them. If you do not have metrics yet, describe the decision and the user outcome.
  • Tailor skills and the summary to the job description. Do not add tools you have only opened once.
  • Keep portfolio links visible in the header and again under projects.
  • Leave out photo, age, marital status, and other personal details that are not needed for the role.
  • Use clear section headings: Summary, Skills, Experience, Projects, Education, Certifications.
  • Keep formatting consistent: one font, simple bullets, and no text inside images so ATS software can read the file.
