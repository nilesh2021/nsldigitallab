Website Launch Checklist

Run this on staging, then again on production. Do not announce the site until the critical items are ticked.

Functionality

  • Every form submits and notifies you Contact, newsletter, and download forms. Test with a real email.
  • Links and CTAs work Nav, footer, buttons, and phone/email links. No leftover localhost URLs.
  • 404 and 500 pages exist Custom 404 with a path back to home or contact.

SEO, analytics & legal

  • Titles, descriptions, and favicon Unique titles on key pages. Correct canonical and sitemap.
  • Analytics and Search Console GA4 (or equivalent) live. Property verified. Conversion events tested.
  • Privacy, cookies, and terms Linked in the footer. Cookie notice matches what you actually load.

Quality & security

  • SSL and domain redirects www/non-www and HTTP/HTTPS resolve to one URL.
  • Mobile, speed, and images Compressed images, alt text, and a passable PageSpeed score on the homepage.
  • Backups and CMS access Hosting backup on, admin 2FA on, and unused plugins/themes removed.
