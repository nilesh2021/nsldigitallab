SEO Interview Questions for Freshers

Practise these answers out loud before an SEO internship or junior executive interview. Short answers beat memorised essays.

Basics

What is SEO?

Search Engine Optimization is the work of helping the right pages appear in search results for people who already want what you offer. It covers content, technical health, and authority.

What is the difference between on-page and off-page SEO?

On-page is what you control on the site: titles, headings, content, internal links, speed. Off-page is signals from other sites, mainly links and mentions.

What is a keyword?

The phrase someone types (or speaks) into Google. You match pages to intent, not just to a word count.

What is search intent?

Why the person searched: to learn, compare, buy, or find a local business. A how-to query wants a guide, not a product page.

Technical and tools

What is Google Search Console?

A free Google tool that shows how your site appears in search, which queries bring clicks, and crawl or index issues.

What is a canonical URL?

The preferred version of a page when similar URLs exist (http/https, www, parameters). It tells Google which URL to index.

What are Core Web Vitals?

Speed and stability measures Google uses, including loading, interactivity, and layout shift. Slow, jumpy pages rank worse and convert worse.

What is local SEO?

Helping a business show up for nearby searches: Google Business Profile, NAP consistency, local pages, and reviews.

Practical / internship

How do you do keyword research as a beginner?

Start from the business offer, list phrases customers would type, check Google autocomplete and related searches, then pick long-tails you can realistically rank for.

How would you improve a blog post that gets impressions but few clicks?

Rewrite the title and meta description to match intent, make the promise specific, and check the snippet against competitors on page one.

What is an XML sitemap?

A file that lists important URLs so search engines can find them. It should contain live canonical pages only.

Questions you can ask the interviewer

Which tools does the team use? Will I work on live sites? How do you measure intern success in 30 days?
