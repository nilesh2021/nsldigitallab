Digital Marketing Planning Checklist

Use this to brief a month of marketing. Fill it once, then review it in your weekly standup.

Goals & offer

  • One primary goal Leads, sales, booked calls, or email signups — pick one number to move.
  • Audience is named Who this is for, what problem they have, and why they would act now.
  • Offer and CTA are clear Landing page, form, or WhatsApp/call path is decided before creative work starts.

Channels

  • Organic search plan Target pages, keywords, and one content piece that supports the offer.
  • Social plan Platforms, posting cadence, and 8–12 post ideas for the month.
  • Paid plan (if using ads) Budget, audience, landing page, and conversion event are defined.
  • Email or remarketing A follow-up for people who saw the offer but did not convert.

Tracking & review

  • UTM links are consistent source / medium / campaign naming is documented.
  • Conversion events work Form submit, call click, or purchase fires in analytics and ads.
  • Monthly review booked What worked, what wasted spend, and what to double next month.
