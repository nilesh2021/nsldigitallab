UI/UX Internship Starter Kit

A 30-day plan, portfolio checklist, and interview prompts for freshers applying to UI/UX internships.

30-day internship prep plan

  • Week 1 — Process and Figma Learn Discovery → Testing. Practise frames, Auto Layout, and components. Clone two app screens.
  • Week 2 — One redesign case study Pick a real app you use. Research 5 users or reviews, wireframe, then UI. Write the problem in 5 lines.
  • Week 3 — Portfolio and resume One case study page, a resume with Figma / research / prototyping keywords, and 3 screenshots with captions.
  • Week 4 — Apply and practise interviews Apply to internships. Practise “tell me about yourself” and walk through your case study out loud.

Portfolio must-haves

  • Problem and who it is for Not “I designed a food app.” Say who struggled and why.
  • Process, not only pretty screens Research, flows, wireframes, UI, test, what you changed.
  • Your role and tools What you did vs a teammate. Figma, FigJam, Maze, or Forms.
  • One measurable or observed result Even “5 students completed checkout in testing” counts.

Interview prompts to practise out loud

Tell me about yourself

Present (who you are) + proof (one project) + fit (why this internship).

Walk me through a case study

Problem, constraints, options you considered, decision, result. Do not only click through Figma.

What is Auto Layout?

A Figma feature that spaces and sizes items when content changes, similar to how developers stack UI.

UI vs UX

UX is the experience and problem-solving. UI is the visual and interactive layer. They work together.

Application checklist

  • Resume is one page Skills: Figma, wireframing, prototyping, user research, Auto Layout, accessibility basics.
  • Portfolio link works on mobile Notion, Behance, or a simple site. No “view on desktop only.”
  • Cover note is 5–7 lines Why this team, which project you would talk about, and that you can start.
