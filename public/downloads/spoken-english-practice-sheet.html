Spoken English Practice Sheet

Use this with the free Spoken English Communication path. Speak out loud. Tick items only after you have recorded them.

Daily 10-minute habit

  • Warm up out loud Read one paragraph. Do not whisper.
  • Speak for four minutes Describe your day, a project, or a news story in your own words.
  • Record 60 seconds Same topic. Finish every sentence.
  • Listen once Write one win and one fix. Stop there.

Tell me about yourself (write, then speak)

Present

Who you are now, in one sentence.

I’m [name], a [student / intern / designer] focusing on [skill].

Proof

One project, internship, or piece of work.

I [built / redesigned / wrote] [project] using [tool], and [small result].

Fit

Why this role, in one sentence.

I’m looking for an internship where I can [real work you want].
