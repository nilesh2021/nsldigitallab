100 ChatGPT Prompts for Job Seekers (2026)
Section 4 — Networking & outreach (prompts 66–80)

66. Write a LinkedIn connection note under 300 characters to a recruiter hiring for [ROLE] at [COMPANY]. No CV dump.

67. Write a LinkedIn connection note under 300 characters to an alumnus from [SCHOOL] who works as [ROLE] at [COMPANY].

68. Draft a polite LinkedIn message asking for a 15-minute career chat. I am a [DEGREE] student targeting [ROLE]. Keep it under 80 words.

69. Rewrite this cold email to a hiring manager. Subject line + 90-word body + one proof point: [PASTE DRAFT] [PASTE PROOF]

70. Write 5 variants of a referral request to a former intern / classmate who works at [COMPANY]. Tone: grateful, specific, easy to say no.

71. Draft a message to a recruiter after I applied on the career site for [JOB ID / ROLE]. 70 words, attach nothing extra, ask if anything else is useful.

72. Write a comment I can leave on a hiring post for [ROLE] that is not “Interested, please check my resume.” Mention one relevant project.

73. Help me ask a professor / mentor for a LinkedIn recommendation. 80 words, plus 4 bullets they could mention: [PASTE ACHIEVEMENTS]

74. Draft an informational-interview follow-up that thanks them and names one action I will take this week: [PASTE NOTES]

75. Write a short “how we met” reminder for someone I spoke with at [EVENT] about [TOPIC], asking to stay in touch.

76. Turn this job post into a 4-line recruiter InMail: who I am, one match, one proof, one ask: [PASTE JD] [PASTE BIO]

77. Rewrite this networking message so it asks for advice, not a job. Remove desperation: [PASTE MESSAGE]

78. Suggest 8 people types I should contact for a [ROLE] search in [CITY], and a one-line reason to reach each.

79. Write a polite decline for an unpaid “exposure” project while keeping the door open for paid [ROLE] work.

80. Draft a 3-message LinkedIn sequence (connect, value, ask) spread over 10 days for reaching a [ROLE] at [COMPANY]. Each under 70 words.
